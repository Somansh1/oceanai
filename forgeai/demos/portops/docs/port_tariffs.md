# Meridian Harbour — Port Tariffs & Operating Rules (Rev. 7, effective 1 Nov 2026)

## Port Dues

Port dues are charged per call at USD 0.85 per gross tonnage (GT), with a minimum charge of USD 4,500 per call. Vessels below 5,294 GT therefore always pay the minimum.

## Berth Occupancy

Berth occupancy is charged at USD 3,200 per day for the first 3 days of a stay and USD 4,800 per day from day 4 onward. Partial days are charged pro-rata by the hour (a day counts as 24 hours from the recorded berthing time).

## Demurrage

Every vessel receives free time at berth before demurrage accrues: 24 hours for dry cargo vessels and 36 hours for tankers (crude and products). After free time expires, demurrage is USD 18,500 per day, pro-rated hourly.

## Pilotage

Pilotage is charged per movement (in or out) by vessel length overall (LOA):
- LOA under 200 m: USD 2,900 per movement
- LOA 200–280 m inclusive: USD 4,100 per movement
- LOA over 280 m: USD 5,600 per movement

## Towage

Two tugs are standard for all movements at USD 1,150 per hour per tug. A third tug is mandatory for vessels over 250 m LOA. Typical movement duration is 2 hours.

## Weather Stoppage Rule

Cargo operations and movements are suspended when sustained wind exceeds 25 m/s or significant swell exceeds 3.5 m at the port approaches (51.95 N, 4.14 E). Time lost during official weather stoppages does not count against demurrage free time; the harbour master logs stoppage windows.

## Noise and Night Restrictions

Tanker operations at OIL1 and OIL2 berths are not permitted between 23:00 and 05:00 local time. Requests for exceptions require the harbour master's written approval.

## Waste Reception

Waste disposal is charged flat at USD 850 per call for vessels under 200 m LOA and USD 1,250 per call at or above 200 m LOA.
