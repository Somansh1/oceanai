# Northwind Maritime Ltd — Shore Staff Leave Policy (v3.2, effective Jan 2025)

## Annual Leave Entitlement

All shore-based staff are entitled to 24 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Staff who joined mid-year receive a pro-rated entitlement of 2 days per completed month of service. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year; anything beyond 5 days expires on 31 March.

Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 10 working days before the intended start date. Your line manager responds within 5 working days. Requests during the July–August peak season require 20 working days notice and may be capped by department staffing plans.

## Sick Leave

Shore staff receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not count against annual leave entitlement. Long-term illness beyond 30 days transfers to company disability insurance.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Parental leave can start up to 2 weeks before the expected due date and must commence within 12 months of birth. Adoption follows the same entitlements.

## Unpaid Leave and Sabbatical

Staff with 3+ years of service may apply for unpaid leave between 2 weeks and 3 months, subject to operational approval. A sabbatical of up to 6 months is available after 7 years of service; only one employee per department may be on sabbatical at a time.

## Public Holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays per year at the registered office location. Staff required to work a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 30 days or double pay, at the company's discretion. Remote staff based in other countries observe their local public holidays instead; the count may differ but total annual entitlement is adjusted to remain equivalent.
