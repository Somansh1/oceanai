# Greystone University Library — Lending Rules (2026–27)

## Loan Periods and Limits

Undergraduate students may borrow up to 8 items at a time for 21 days. Postgraduate students may borrow 12 items for 28 days. Academic staff may borrow 20 items for 56 days. External members are limited to 4 items for 14 days. Reference materials and current journal issues cannot leave the building.

## Renewals

Loans can be renewed up to 3 times if no other patron has placed a hold on the item. Renewals extend the loan by the original period. Items with a hold cannot be renewed, online or at the desk.

## Fines and Fees

Overdue standard items accrue fines of 30 pence per item per day, capped at £15 per item. Short-loan items (4-hour loans) accrue £1 per hour with no cap. Lost items are charged at the replacement cost plus a £10 administrative fee. Fines stop accruing once a replacement charge is issued. Patrons owing more than £10 cannot borrow until the balance is cleared.

## Holds and Recalls

Any patron can place a hold on a loaned item; when the item is returned it is held at the service desk for 4 days. If a lecturer recalls an item needed for a course, the loan period shortens to 7 days from the recall notice, and recall notices are sent by university email.

## Inter-Library Loans

Items not held by Greystone can be requested through inter-library loan: 2 requests per patron per month free of charge, £3 per request thereafter. Delivery typically takes 5–10 working days. Inter-library loans cannot be renewed.

## Special Collections

Special collections material is consultable in the reading room only, Monday to Friday 09:00–17:00, by appointment made at least 2 working days ahead. Photography for personal study is permitted; publication requires written permission from the special collections librarian.
